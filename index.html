Every day, thousands of parcels pass through Thimmalapura — sorted, connected, delivered. But on Monday, 9 November, the most precious delivery was different: a red box of sweets, a bag of fruit, and a few unhurried minutes together. Banana stems went up at the bay, marigolds looped over a green silk backdrop, and right in the middle of the warehouse floor, the hub held its Diwali pooja.

✿
Chapter 01 · The Pooja

A temple, right where the trucks dock

No hall was booked and no stage was built. The team simply claimed a corner of the hub floor and made it sacred — the way this team does everything: together, and with whatever is at hand.

Banana stems, marigold & a green silk sky

Two tall banana stems (baale kamba) framed the pandal, exactly as tradition asks. Over a green patterned silk cloth hung loops of yellow marigold and fresh green garlands, with little red blooms tucked in between.

On the black-draped table: bananas, apples, coconut, flowers and the pooja thali — and on the floor, the small brass lamps and steel tumblers of a ceremony done properly, shoes off, hearts in.

"Sort floor aaytu, sanctum aaytu — same floor, full bhakti!"
🌼 Marigold toranam🌿 Banana stems🍌 Fruit offerings🪔 Brass deepas

Bare feet on the hub floor

Look closely at every photo from the morning and you'll notice the same thing — everyone is barefoot. Inside the pooja space, designations stayed outside with the footwear. Team leads, executives, associates, security — one circle, no ranks.

Between the aarti and the photographs, there was laughter, teasing, and that particular hub-floor noise that no celebration can quite silence — nor would anyone want it to.

🤝 No ranks inside the pandal😄 Full masti
🪔
Chapter 02 · Sweets & Smiles

The sweetest handover of the season

This hub hands over thousands of shipments a day. On Diwali, the handover that mattered was a bright red sweet box and a green bag of fruit — given by hand, received with both hands, and sealed with a photograph.

One box, one bag, one big smile at a time

One by one, every member of the team stepped up to the pandal to receive their Diwali sweet box and fruit bag. Each handover got its own moment — a handshake, a grin for the camera, and the pooja backdrop glowing behind them.

It is a small ritual, but it carries a big message that this hub runs on: nobody here is just a headcount. Every person who sorts, drives, guards, solves and supervises through the year was called up by turn and celebrated.

"Parcel delivery daily ide… sweet box delivery, swalpa special!"
🎁 Sweet boxes for all🍎 Fruit bags📷 A photo for everyone

Even the pallet jack got dressed up

Only at Thimmalapura would the hand pallet truck wear marigolds for the occasion — decorated and parked right beside the pandal like an honoured guest. The tools of the trade were part of the pooja too, blessed for another year of safe, smooth runs.

Petals scattered across the concrete, the smell of flowers mixing with the everyday hub air — for a few hours, the warehouse smelled like a festival, because it was one.

🏵️ Ayudha pooja spirit🚚 Tools blessed too

Chapter 03 · One Parivaara

Shifts change. This family doesn't.

A, B and C shifts. Hub, FM and LM. PST solving problems in the corner, security watching over it all. Diwali was the rare morning the whole parivaara stood in one place at one time — and the camera could barely fit everyone in.

Twenty smiles, one frame, zero space left

The big group photos say everything: rows of teammates shoulder to shoulder, a couple crouched in front, the pandal peeking out from behind. This is the crew that keeps Tumkur's parcels moving through peak season, rain, and 2 AM connects alike.

Festivals at a 24×7 hub are never easy to pull off — someone is always on shift, someone always on the road. That this many people could gather, dress up, and celebrate together is its own small Diwali miracle.

"A shift, B shift, C shift — Diwali ge ella ondu shift!"
